How it works

Short lessons that stick.

Read, then prove it

Every lesson is a few short paragraphs in plain English, followed by a multiple-choice check. Get it right first time and you earn full points; miss it, re-read, and a late correct answer still counts.

Points, ranks and streaks

Lessons, checks and answer streaks all earn points that climb five ranks. A daily streak counts every day you learn something, and finishing a course earns a certificate.

Video courses & simulations

Filmed walkthroughs and a full trading simulation are in the works. The articles, quick reviews and unit tests are live today.
